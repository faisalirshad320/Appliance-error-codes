# Appliance Error Codes Niche: Validation Report

Date: 2026-10-15
Market analysed: United States, Google organic
Data source: Ahrefs API (keyword volumes, keyword difficulty, SERP overviews, competitor domain metrics, traffic history). Semrush and Firecrawl were out of credits, so no second-source cross-check was possible. Raw pulls are in the `data/` folder.

## 1. Verdict in one paragraph

Yes, the niche is real and worth building on, but with two corrections to the original brief. First, the demand is large but smaller than "500k+/mo": the top 250 US keywords in the brand × appliance × code family add up to roughly 175k searches per month, and the whole family including every phrasing is plausibly 350k to 500k, but those phrasings collapse into about 1,000 to 2,000 unique pages. Second, the easy part of the SERP is positions 5 to 10, not positions 1 to 3. Keyword difficulty is 0 to 5 on almost every term and DR 11 to 18 sites hold page-one spots today, but the top three spots are held by the manufacturer support page, Reddit, YouTube and an AI Overview on 100% of the keywords checked. A new site can reach page one in 4 to 6 months. Reaching position 1 to 3 consistently is hard.

## 2. Is it worth building a website on it?

Yes, with the right monetisation. Evidence:

| Signal | What the data says |
|---|---|
| Keyword difficulty | 0 to 5 out of 100 on 36 of 38 sampled terms. "whirlpool washer error codes" is KD 3, "lg washer ue code" is KD 0. |
| Low-DR sites on page one | wilsonmyers.co (DR 12) ranks #6 for "bosch dishwasher e15" (2,400/mo). highcityappliance.com (DR 11) ranks #9 for the same term. beaconsaves.com (DR 18) ranks #7 for "lg dryer d80 code" (4,500/mo) and #5 for "samsung washer ub". hoffmannbros.com (DR 44) ranks #6 for "lg washer ue code" (5,200/mo). |
| Speed to traffic | wilsonmyers.co went from 0 to 7,768 organic visits/month in 8 months (Aug 2025 to Oct 2026), with no large link profile. beaconsaves.com went from 230 to 61,386 visits/month in 7 months (Dec 2025 to Jul 2026). |
| Search intent | Pure problem-solving. Someone with a flashing "4C" on their washer needs an answer in the next five minutes. This converts very well to parts, repair bookings and home-warranty leads. |
| Dead competitors | The dedicated niche sites that used to own this (applianceanalysts.com DR 42, errorcodespro.com DR 23, appliancerepair.net, washererrorcodes.com) all show zero organic traffic now. The space has been vacated. |

Two warnings that affect the "yes":

- **AI Overviews are on every keyword checked.** Clicks-per-search sits at 0.3 to 0.9 on the big code terms, meaning 10% to 70% of searches end with no click at all. Ahrefs' traffic potential for the #1 page is routinely under half the keyword volume (for example 5,200 searches for "lg washer ue code" but only 4,300 visits/month to the #1 page across all its keywords).
- **Volatility is high.** beaconsaves.com peaked at 61k in July 2026 and has fallen to 23.5k in October 2026, a 62% drop in three months. This is the pattern of a site hit by a core update. Expect the same swings.

## 3. How much potential does the niche have?

### Demand size (US)

| Slice | Monthly searches | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Top 100 "brand + appliance + code" keywords | ~96,000 | Mostly Samsung and LG washers and dishwashers |
| Top 250 keywords | ~175,000 | 250th keyword is still 250/mo, so the tail is long |
| Whole family, all phrasings (estimate) | 350,000 to 500,000 | Many phrasings of the same code ("lg washer ue code", "ue code on lg washer", "ue error lg washer") |
| Unique topics (one page each) | ~1,000 to 2,000 | What a site actually has to publish |

Global volume is typically only 5% to 30% higher than US for Samsung/LG/Whirlpool terms, and about 80% higher for Bosch (strong UK and EU demand). This is a US-first niche.

### Where the demand concentrates

| Brand | Share of top-250 volume (approx) | Highest-volume codes |
|---|---|---|
| LG | ~38% | washer UE, OE, IE, LE, CL, DE; dryer D80, D90; dishwasher AE, CL, BE |
| Samsung | ~33% | washer 4C, SC, UR, SUD, SE, UB, U6, DC, 4E, ND, NF; dishwasher LC, OC; dryer HC, DC |
| Whirlpool / Maytag | ~12% | F21, 5D, F5 E3, F8 E4, LF, CL; brand-level "error codes" list pages |
| Bosch | ~8% | dishwasher E15, E24, E25, E22, E23 |
| GE, KitchenAid, Frigidaire, Kenmore, Speed Queen | ~9% | mostly list pages, under 1,000/mo each |

Two page types exist and they rank differently:

1. **Single-code pages** ("samsung washer 4c code"). Highest volume per page, KD 0, but the manufacturer page, Reddit and YouTube take positions 1 to 4. Realistic landing spot for a new site is 5 to 10.
2. **Brand list pages** ("whirlpool washer error codes", 2,700/mo). The manufacturer owns three of the top five, but mrappliance.com (DR 66) and Sears Home Services sit at 7 and 9 with only 1 referring domain each. Easier to hold position 5 to 8 and they feed internal links to every code page.

Rule of thumb: a code page on a DR 10 to 20 site, ranked 5 to 10, brings 150 to 600 visits/month. A site with 300 such pages where a third are on page one would be at 15k to 40k visits/month.

### Ceiling

If the site reached the traffic of the strongest independent ranker in the space (hoffmannbros.com, 17.6k visits/month on appliance content, Ahrefs value $39.7k/mo), that would be a top-decile outcome. beaconsaves.com's 61k peak shows the ceiling is higher with 1,000+ pages and aggressive publishing, but it also shows the drawdown risk.

## 4. Revenue after 6 months

Assumptions: new domain, no existing links, 200 to 400 pages published by month 6 (programmatic or semi-programmatic, one page per brand × appliance × code plus brand list pages), US traffic, launch in month 1.

Display RPMs for the DIY/home-improvement niche in 2026 vary by source: roughly $7 to $16 per 1,000 sessions on entry networks (AdSense, Ezoic), $20 to $35 on Mediavine or Raptive (sources below). Raptive needs 25k pageviews/month, Mediavine 50k sessions, so a six-month-old site is on entry networks unless it hits the upside case.

| Scenario | Organic visits/mo at month 6 | Display ads | Affiliate (parts, warranty, repair leads) | Total/month |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low (slow indexing, Google sandbox, AI Overview losses) | 2,000 to 5,000 | $15 to $60 | $20 to $80 | **$35 to $140** |
| Base (tracks wilsonmyers.co trajectory, full-time focus on this niche) | 8,000 to 15,000 | $80 to $250 | $100 to $400 | **$180 to $650** |
| High (tracks beaconsaves.co trajectory, 500+ pages, strong internal linking, Raptive approved) | 25,000 to 45,000 | $500 to $1,300 | $400 to $1,200 | **$900 to $2,500** |

The most likely answer is $200 to $600 per month at month 6, growing to $1,000 to $3,000 per month by month 12 to 18 if rankings hold. The first six months are mostly investment.

Where the money actually is, in order:

1. **Repair lead generation.** Every low-DR site ranking here is a service business (wilsonmyers.co is a Denver repair company, hoffmannbros.com is a St. Louis contractor, beaconsaves.com is a home services and warranty company). Ahrefs values a single code page at $250 to $300/month in equivalent PPC spend. Pay-per-lead programs (Angi, Networx, HomeAdvisor affiliates) pay $10 to $40 per repair lead. A "still showing the code? book a technician" box on every page beats display ads.
2. **Parts affiliate.** PartSelect, RepairClinic and AppliancePartsPros run 6% to 8% programs. A 4C code usually means a $20 to $60 inlet valve or filter; an E15 often needs a $30 to $90 pump or float switch. Conversion of 0.5% to 1.5% of visitors is realistic because intent is urgent.
3. **Home warranty affiliate.** $30 to $60 per quote lead. Fits naturally on pages about expensive failures (control boards, compressors).
4. **Display ads.** Baseline income, not the business.

Sources for ad benchmarks: [earnifyhub RPM by niche 2026](https://earnifyhub.com/blog/blogging/blog-display-ad-rpm-by-niche-2026), [earnifyhub Mediavine estimates](https://www.earnifyhub.com/blog/affiliate/mediavine-vs-affiliate-marketing-priority), [Mediavine RPM definition](https://mediavine.com/mediavine-rpm), [Flippa listing of a Raptive DIY site](https://flippa.com/13711902-home-improvement-site-us-rpm-55-yoy-21-5k-ltm-net-31-mo-costs-pinterest-led-full-2026-traffic-data-disclosed-raptive).

## 6. Easy or hard to rank?

**Page one (positions 5 to 10): easy by SEO standards. Positions 1 to 3: hard.**

Why page one is reachable:

- Keyword difficulty 0 to 5 across the board.
- Pages ranking 6 to 10 typically have 1 to 4 referring domains and URL rating 0 to 6. You do not need links to individual pages, only a modestly trusted domain.
- Two DR 11 to 12 sites are on page one for a 2,400/mo term right now.
- Existing content is weak: manufacturer pages are generic, JustAnswer pages are thin, Reddit threads are unstructured.

Why the top three is hard:

- Position 1 is an AI Overview, position 2 or 3 is the manufacturer, position 3 or 4 is Reddit. That is structural, not a content-quality problem.
- Traffic potential of the #1 organic page is typically 40% to 80% of keyword volume, so even a win gives less than the headline number.
- Core-update volatility (beaconsaves lost 62% in three months) is the main risk to a programmatic site in this space.

What it takes to win the available slots:

1. **Match the URL and content pattern that already works.** `/brand/appliance/code/` with a fixed structure: what the code means, the three most common causes in order of likelihood, step-by-step fix with photos, when to call a technician, which part to order. hoffmannbros.com does exactly this at DR 44 and earns 17.6k visits/month.
2. **Be more specific than the manufacturer.** Model-series differences, the "I cleaned the filter and it still shows 4C" follow-up (Reddit wins these today), and the exact part numbers.
3. **Cover the whole brand before moving on.** LG and Samsung washers and dishwashers are 70% of the volume. 150 pages covering every LG and Samsung code plus the brand list pages would address roughly 120k searches/month.
4. **Earn 20 to 50 referring domains to the domain in the first six months.** Enough to get DR 15 to 20, which is where beaconsaves and wilsonmyers rank from. Appliance-repair directories, local business listings, HARO-style quotes, and a free "error code lookup" tool are the realistic link sources.
5. **Add first-hand signals.** Original photos of the error on the display, the part removed, a short video per code. This is what separates a page from JustAnswer and what AI Overviews cite.
6. **Monetise for leads, not just ads.** Tier 3 sites prove the ranking model, and they are all service businesses. Build the "book a repair" and "order this part" paths from day one.
