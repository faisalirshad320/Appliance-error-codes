Finding the right part

The single most expensive mistake in appliance repair is ordering a part for the wrong model. Error codes are shared across platforms; parts almost never are.

Find your model number first

Washers and dryers: inside the door frame, or on the back panel. Dishwashers: on the edge of the inner door, visible when the door is open. Refrigerators: inside, usually on a side wall or behind a crisper drawer. Ovens and ranges: on the frame behind the door, or in a drawer.

Confirm the part before you buy

Check the part number printed on the component you are replacing against the listing, not just the model compatibility list. Manufacturers revise parts mid-production and a supersession can mean the part that fits your serial range is not the one the chart shows.

Test before you replace

Most of the components error codes point at can be tested with a multimeter for a few dollars of effort rather than guessed at for a hundred. Each page says what reading to expect where that is knowable. Swapping parts until the code clears is the expensive way to repair an appliance.
