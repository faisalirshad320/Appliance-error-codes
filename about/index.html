About and how we source this

This site exists because error-code pages are usually bad. The manufacturer page tells you the code means "water supply error" and stops. A forum thread gives you nine conflicting answers. A content farm gives you six paragraphs of preamble before the one sentence you needed.

What we do differently

Every page leads with the answer. Causes are ordered by how often they actually occur rather than presented as an undifferentiated list. Every fix step names the tool, the part and roughly how long it takes. And every page has a section for the case where the obvious fix did not work, because that is where most people actually are by the time they are searching.

Where the information comes from

Each page lists its sources. In practice that means manufacturer support documentation and service literature for what a code signifies, and the repair community for what actually fixes it in the field. Where sources disagree, the page says so rather than picking one silently.

Diagrams

Every diagram is original work, drawn as vector graphics specifically for this site. None are scanned, traced or copied from a service manual or a product photograph. They are schematic rather than photographic on purpose: when you are looking for a drain filter behind a kick panel, a labelled drawing of where it sits beats a glamour shot of the appliance.

Corrections

If a page is wrong, it should be fixed. Appliance platforms change between model years and a procedure that is right for one series can be wrong for the next. Tell us which page, which model and what actually happened.
