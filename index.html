How this site works

Every page answers the same four questions in the same order: what the code means, why it happens, what to do about it, and what to do when that did not work. The last one is the question most pages on this subject never answer, and it is usually the one that matters.

Diagrams are drawn for this site rather than taken from manufacturer manuals, so they show the part you actually need to find rather than an exploded parts view. Costs are typical United States figures. Where a job involves mains wiring, gas or a sealed refrigerant circuit, the page says so and tells you to stop.
